## Comparison and standard error

All means below are aggregate counts divided by the 1,000 levels at that depth; Delta is Port minus C. For monster/gold count rates I used the Poisson-rate approximation SE(mean) = sqrt(mean / N). For object/monster feeling bins I used the binomial approximation SE(mean) = sqrt(p(1-p) / N). The combined SE is sqrt(SE_C squared + SE_Port squared), treating the two independent 1,000-run samples as independent. This is an approximation because the SQLite schema stores aggregate bins rather than per-run observations, and gold values can be overdispersed.

For the ranked list, a metric is called beyond noise only when it exceeds both the unchanged parity envelope max(2, 5% of |C mean|) and 3 combined SE; this avoids promoting sparse-bin fluctuations and preserves the existing tolerance for review. There are 57 such metrics.
